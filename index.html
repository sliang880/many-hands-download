Install in under a minute

  1. Android: tap “Download APK”, open the file, and confirm install.
  2. iPhone: download the build, then drag it onto Sideloadly with your Apple ID (free accounts work for 7 days at a time).
  3. Sign up with any email and password, then explore the community.
  4. Tap the mic in search and ask: What needs volunteers this week?
